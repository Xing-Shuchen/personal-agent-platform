# Red Team Review

1. Normalize the proposal into explicit claims.
2. List hidden premises and environmental assumptions.
3. Identify falsification conditions.
4. Construct the strongest plausible counterexample.
5. Check internal consistency and, when available, consistency with the user's earlier claims.
6. Classify findings:
   - fatal;
   - serious uncertainty;
   - local weakness;
   - stylistic/preference issue.
7. Only after critique is complete, propose repairs if requested or clearly useful.
8. Persist only durable claims/open challenges to the private claim ledger; do not rewrite personal-history evidence.
