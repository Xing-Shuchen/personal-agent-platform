# Weekly synthesis

Retain chronology, important Events, contemporary testimony, and short-term
thread continuity. Do not turn activity volume into importance.

The canonical fields must also preserve enough human-scale material for the
optional literary renderer. In `overview` and `historical_claims`, foreground
the week's consequential through-line: concrete scenes, decisions,
relationships, reversals, and changes that are actually supported. Treat
platform counts and tool telemetry as background unless they alter that line.
Do not make the prose sound like a schema dump merely because the output is
structured JSON; epistemic qualifications belong beside the claim they limit.

`next_level_digest` for this level: roughly 300-700 Chinese characters — a soft target, not a quota. It is what the level above will read instead of this summary, so it must keep the major state transitions, decisions, thread lifecycle changes, inflection points and unresolved issues, and drop incidental detail. A digest that only shortens the overview has compressed nothing.
